Château de Cheverny

Loir-et-Cher · 1624–1630

About the model

Cheverny was built between 1624 and 1630 for Philippe Hurault, in white tuffeau limestone from Bourré, which bleaches as it weathers. The two end pavilions carry dômes à l’impériale with open lanterns; the court front is banded ashlar set with busts of Roman emperors, while the garden front is rendered panels in a stone frame, with a dry moat at its feet.

Hergé drew it as Marlinspike Hall in The Adventures of Tintin — with the two end pavilions removed.

Everything you see is generated at load: no mesh files, no textures and no libraries. The stone coursing, the slate, the gravel and the leaves are all noise functions evaluated in the fragment shader.
